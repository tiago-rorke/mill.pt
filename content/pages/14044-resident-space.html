A Space for Residents

A Place to Make

Besides the nice company, being a resident gives you access to a lot of space, machines, knowledge and help.

The Desks

Residents have their own desk, to keep personal stuff, computers and ongoing projects parts and pieces.

Full Makerspace Access

Being a resident, you can make full use of the machines and tools, and are free to keep your own schedule with 24h access to the space.

Part of a collective

Being a resident also means that you’ll get to know the other residents, and (probably) get involved in different – and unexpected – technologies and ways of thinking and feeling the world around us.

Good amenities

Besides lots of work tools and spaces, we also have a reading-in-the-sun corner, a little kitchen, WCs, and you can park your bike inside! What more can you want?
