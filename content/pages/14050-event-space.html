A Space For Events

A Place for Making Friends

We host a range of events at MILL – from workshops to meetups and exhibitions, it is a place for meeting, sharing knowledge, and starting new collaborations.

If you think MILL would be the perfect place for your event, please contact us with your idea. To see what kind of activities have been happening at MILL, have a look at our previous events.

Public

If you would like to add a new event to our calendar, we would be happy to host you! We can help provide tools and materials depending on the activity, and our fees are flexible depending on the nature of the event and the resources needed.

Private

Wether you are a corporate team, school class or birthday party, we can provide workshops for private groups in the areas of electronics, programming, digital fabrication and more.  If we don't have an in-house solution for you, we can also try to find someone from our community who can teach you the particular skills or tools you are looking for.
