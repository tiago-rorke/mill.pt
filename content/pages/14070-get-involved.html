Joining

Get involved

MILL is a place for connecting and collaborating with people from the local, and international, maker communities. There are many different options for getting involved at MILL, such as temporary residencies and collaborations, or becoming a full time resident and/or member of our association.

If you are interested in finding out more about MILL we recommend coming along to the monthly meetup, or you can also get in touch to schedule a visit.

Join as a resident

Are you looking for a workspace and would like to be part of the Lisbon maker community? MILL residents have 24-hour access to the makerspace with dedicated work and storage space in a creative and collaborative environment.

Find out more about our facilities for residents here.

Become a Collaborator

So you aren’t looking for a full-time workspace, but still want to participate at MILL?  Whether you are looking for assistance on a project, or just want to help out and learn some new skills, we accommodate all sorts of collaborations.

Become an Association Member

After more than 10 years of existing as an informal collective, we formally became the cultural association MILL - MAKERS IN LITTLE LISBON ASSOCIAÇÃO CULTURAL at the beginning of 2026, allowing us to give and receive grants, and formally collaborate with other institutions as a Portuguese legal entity.

You can become member to benefit from the structure of the association in your own work, as well as to support our makerspace, and participate in the development of our community.

You can register as a member by filling out this form, and paying an annual membership fee.

Donate Something

We also love breathing new life into old things and are always finding new opportunities for unwanted equipment.

If you have tech, tools or materials that are looking for a new home please get in touch.  As an independent and self-funded space, financial support is also a big help!
